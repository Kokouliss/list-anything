# Audit: List Anything (Android, Compose)

2026-11-16, on `a4e7a65` (main after PR #16). Read from the code and from the screens drawn on the JVM with sample data; nothing ran on a phone. OpenDesigner's page probes (`review.py`, `probe.js`, `audit-page`) read web pages and do not run on an Android app, so every number is measured from `Color.kt` or read at the line given. The readable copy, with the before screens, is the Claude Doc "List Anything design audit".

**Verdict.** The look is in good shape, and three things are wrong: chip borders are too faint to count as the edge of a control (2.1:1 to 2.7:1), the main action is a yellow floating button on a full screen and a blue filled button on an empty one, and the photo viewer is pure black while the bubbles write in pure white.

## Checked and fine
Text contrast: every pair in both themes >= 4.5:1 (lowest 4.85:1 tertiary on paper; 6.61:1 at night); bubble text lowest 4.98:1; `ColorContrastTest` keeps it. One filled button per screen state; opposite actions on one row. Three greys. Two typefaces with named jobs (sans interface, serif headings); weights 400 and 500 (Material default). Six text sizes 12-28 sp. Corners 6/10/14/20 dp from the theme plus circles. Touch: icon buttons and pens 48 dp; the chip x is 30 dp (Material's input chip spec allows 24). Chip labels one line. Loading, empty, error and success states exist. Full dark theme. Hard-coded colours all by design (logo tile ink, white drawing sheet, QR black on white, margin and ruled lines at 0.4/0.25/0.08 by the owner's choice). No endless animation. No Material 2.

## Not measured
Greek labels at large text size (wrapping), touch and TalkBack on a phone, the map screen and Google sign-in sheet (cannot be drawn on the JVM), and OpenDesigner's web probes.
